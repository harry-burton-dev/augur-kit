`ThingMap` and its remote element `quill-thing-map`: the map of what is around a thing, one step away. The thing sits in the middle and its neighbours round it, each group side by side and each line read from the thing's side ("Operated by"), laid out by a fixed rule (`mapAround`) so the same thing always looks the same. Past `cap` (12) it says how many more and opens them in everything we know; in a narrow pane it folds into its groups as lists. A thing's page already offers it through `map`.
