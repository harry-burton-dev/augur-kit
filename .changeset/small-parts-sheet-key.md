Small parts in the picked looks (6 Nov 2026). Menus and tips are drawn as Sheet, like the shell's start menu: a solid square sheet with one edge, a hard ink line on the row you're on, and no blur. Menu gains an optional `heading` and `defaultOpen`, and Tip gains `defaultOpen`. Tabs are drawn as Key, raised keys in a tray. New Common parts `Badge` (a count or word as a small key) and `Divider` (a groove, with a label, strong or upright). Quill's Tag and Card are drawn as keys. The list under Select loses its blur.
