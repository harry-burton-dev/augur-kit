Tiles take PDR-010's last two amendments. A document tile is a dimmer page, so documents no longer always win the eye over the places around them (amendment 4). A tile with a radial menu has a "more" mark, bottom right, that shows while the tile is hovered or holds focus (always on a touch screen) and opens the same menu (amendment 6). The mark sits beside the tile's button in a new `.quill-tile-place` wrapper, which is now the grid item.
