Connection and AppUnavailable drawn as Line (picked 6 Nov 2026). Connection is a full-width line under the band with the state's name and sentence in mono; the line is dashed offline, runs while reconnecting (still when motion is reduced) and breaks off when signed out. It gets an additive `names` prop for the state names. AppUnavailable drops the mark tile: the app's name stands on a horizon line, the state in mono over it with the app's icon, the reason in mono under it; the line is red when it failed and Common's Progress while it opens. New story pages Aerie/Connection and Aerie/App unavailable replace the old ones under Aerie/Notices.
