Writing and reading in the picked looks (6 Nov 2026). The Editor's format bar is a small Sheet of keys over where the selection starts, and a mention is a square chip in the Key look with its kind's icon, in the Editor and in Prose; the mention list is a Sheet. Reader takes `flow` (`scroll`, `leaves` set out as pages that turn and never scroll, or `horizon` with all but your section dimmed), `spread` and `onLeaf`, and its handle `turn`, `leaf` and `leafOf`. Contents takes `leaves` to show each heading's page as a ledger. New `ReadingView` puts them together: Turn pages or Scroll, Page or Desk, switched by the reader and remembered with `remember`. The directions page goes.
