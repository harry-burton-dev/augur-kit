Adds `DoorGallery`, `Door`, `DoorLight`, `AskLine` and `StepLabel`: an ask line on top and a row of doors, each lit by how much waits behind it. Adding a door is one more descriptor, with no change to a part. The light is ink, and green only for needs you; it is still under reduced motion and on a deployment that draws no blur. Every door's name says its job, its step and its reasons. Also adds `useInRing`, so a part can tell whether a radial menu is round it.
