The pickers in the Rail look Harry picked (round M), drawn on one ruled line like page frame A. FilterBar is one ruled line with saved views at its start (new `views`, `view` and `onView` props) and what each filter has on in words, so the row of chips under it is gone. Palette is a ruled strip that dims what's behind it and never blurs it (new `lead` and `found` props). The DateField calendar is a ruled month with the chosen day underlined in ink instead of green. TagPicker writes its tags as ruled words, and Common's Segmented puts an ink bar under the chosen word. List gets a `plain` look and expanding rows (`more` on an item, `moreLabel`). FilterBar, DataView and Palette get their own story pages.
