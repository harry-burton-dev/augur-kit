The desktop bar never runs past the edge, however narrow the screen (WCAG 2.2 reflow at 320 px). More takes the open apps that don't fit as well as the groups, groups first, and lights and leads for what's under it; on a folder bar, Apps takes them. More always holds two things or more. `desktopFit` says what gets a tile. On a pane narrower than pane-tight-below, a split's columns count their padding in their width, so a stacked column stays inside the pane; wider panes keep their widths.
