People and activity in the picked looks (6 Nov 2026). Person and People are drawn as a Disc: a flat grey disc with Instrument Sans initials, ringed in the floor's colour, and you ringed in plain ink, never green. New `here` prop on Person and People: someone away is dimmed back and it's read aloud. New in Common: `Presence` (who is here now), `Comments` (points that stay open until settled, settled ones fold to a line, replies one step in), `Activity` (what needs you pulled above, a find field, a line where you last looked, pages instead of scrolling) and `Notifications` with `NotificationItem` (key rows in a well, unread raised). Quill's PeoplePicker gathers its choices under the field as a list in a well, each with what they can do and a key to take it off.
