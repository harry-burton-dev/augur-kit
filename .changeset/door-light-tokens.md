Adds the `door` token group for Quill's door gallery: the light that rises from a door by how much waits behind it. `door-light-present`, `door-light-strong` and `door-light-needs` are its strengths (numbers, used as percentages in `color-mix`), `door-light-reach-present` and `door-light-reach` how far up it reaches, `door-light-swell` and `door-light-spill` what focus does to it, and `door-drift-strength`, `door-bloom` and `door-bloom-focus` how much it moves at rest. Nothing is renamed or removed.
