Common gains five parts for a place to work in that never scrolls: SectionDialog (a small dialog with its sections as tabs down the side, no primary action), FitParts (parts side by side while each has `part-min-width`, the rest behind a switch), CheckList and CheckRow (things checked, the one that needs you lit from its edge), StepList (an ordered way through one job) and PagedList (a page at a time, Newer and Older). New structure tokens: `dialog-section-width`, `dialog-section-height`, `section-tabs-width` and `part-min-width`.
