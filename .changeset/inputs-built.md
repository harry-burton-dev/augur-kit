New inputs, picked from the input directions. Common: `ComboBox` (type to find one in a long list, its matches on a sheet), `MultiSelect` (several from a set, words and a count in the well), `NumberField` (step keys at the end, a unit in small capitals), `Slider` (a fader, or `steps`), and a `minus` icon. Quill: `TimeField` (written as you'd say it, with an echo of the time it reads) and `DateRangeField` (two date fields joined by a line, with the days it spans).
