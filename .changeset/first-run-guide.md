First-run guide, in the Lamp look: `Guide` shows an app's parts one at a time (the page dims, the part stays lit, the words sit on the dim) with Next, Back and Esc to skip, and `FirstSteps` is a short checklist section for an app's start page. Every word is a prop. Their stories sit in a new Patterns area of the Storybook.
