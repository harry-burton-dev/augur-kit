Aerie draws the frame round an app on the app contract's third major: a command palette over the commands apps declare (Ctrl+Shift+P), the radial menu on the things an app marks with `data-thing`, the rail's places with what an app puts under them (capped, 50 unless the deployment says, with "and n more"), and the About panel on the right. Commands an app can't run yet stay, locked, with the reason. Common gains the `about-panel` and `rail-under` shell tokens.
